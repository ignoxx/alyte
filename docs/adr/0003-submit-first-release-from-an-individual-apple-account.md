# Submit the first release from an individual Apple account

Alyte will first be submitted by its solo German developer through an individual Apple
Developer membership. A company will not be formed solely as a prerequisite for the first
submission, and advance written clearance from Apple is not required before implementation
continues.

This accepts a material App Review risk: Apple's guidelines say healthcare apps or apps requiring
sensitive user information should be submitted by the legal entity providing the service. An
individual membership also publishes the developer's legal name, and EU trader disclosures require
verified public contact information. The product must not obscure its health-data functionality or
publisher identity during review.

If Apple rejects the submission on publisher-entity grounds, the fallback is to form or use an
eligible legal entity, obtain the required organization credentials, convert or transfer the Apple
membership where permitted, and resubmit. German business registration, tax, trader-disclosure,
privacy, and support obligations still apply independently of the Apple membership type.
